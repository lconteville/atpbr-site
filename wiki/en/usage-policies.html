Usage policies

Chapter 13 of 14 · Updated 18 Nov 2026

Access to ATPBR data, whether open through the Results browser or controlled (see Data access), is subject to the policies described below. They complement the principles of de-identification and compliance with the LGPD (Brazilian General Data Protection Law) presented in About ATPBR and the rules of Data governance.

User responsibilities#

Authorized users undertake to:

  • Use the data exclusively for the approved research, within the approved timeframe and under the approved conditions;
  • Not attempt to identify participants, their relatives or the institutions of origin, nor combine ATPBR data with other sources for that purpose;
  • Not contact participants on the basis of the records received;
  • Not share credentials, not make copies available to unauthorized persons and not retransmit the data to third parties;
  • Not use the data for unauthorized purposes, including clinical care or diagnostic purposes. Commercial use is only permitted when participants' consent allows it (datasets without the DUO codes NCU or NPU), under the same access rules, with no exclusivity or patent over the data and with benefit sharing; exceptional cases are decided by the Data Access Committee (CAD) together with the National Governance Council (CGN);
  • Not use the data for forensic or police identification, for risk selection or pricing by insurers or employers, for targeted advertising, or to support racial, ethnic or ancestry hierarchies;
  • Not remove security controls or transfer the data to unauthorized environments;
  • Adopt security measures proportionate to the risks, such as individual authentication, limited privileges, protection of credentials, use of the data exclusively within the ATPBR secure instance and no attempt to extract individual-level data;
  • Respect the restrictions set at origin, which continue to apply to derived data, as well as any publication moratoria;
  • Not publish results that allow participants to be identified, directly or indirectly. Authorization to analyze the data is not authorization to publish individual records;
  • Cite ATPBR and the source studies, acknowledging the centers that contributed samples and data, in publications and products resulting from the use of the data (the reference article is in About ATPBR).

Users of controlled-access data also undertake to:

  • Report to INCA, through the channel and within the deadline set out in the data use agreement, security incidents and any suspected re-identification or misuse;
  • Give prior notice of relevant changes to the purpose, methods, team, participating institutions, processing environment or requested datasets, which may require a new decision by the Data Access Committee;
  • At the end of the access period or upon revocation of access, delete or return the data, or keep them only in the authorized form, and confirm this to ATPBR.

Failure to comply with these conditions may result in the suspension or revocation of access and in other measures provided for in the data use agreement and in ATPBR policies. Applying these measures is the responsibility of the Data Access Committee (CAD), and the decisions are recorded.

Access to data does not include access to the corresponding biological samples, whose governance falls under the National Tumor Bank (BNT) and is not regulated by ATPBR.

Transparency and traceability#

Requests, decisions, institutional sign-offs, enablements, accesses, suspensions, revocations and closures are recorded, with protection against unauthorized alteration, for the purposes of auditing, traceability and compliance with ATPBR governance policies (see Data governance). A public list of approved requests, with the project title, the institution and the authorized datasets, is published periodically on the ATPBR website.
