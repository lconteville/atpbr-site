Data types

Version 0.1 · Chapter 10 of 14 · Updated 18 Nov 2026

The previous chapters dealt with metadata: how they are organized (Data model), how they are filled in (Data dictionary) and how they generate the identifiers (Identification and barcode system). This chapter deals with formats: what ATPBR accepts at submission and what the platform generates from it.

Overview#

ATPBR accepts two types of data for submission:

Category Accepted format
Metadata Portal forms or Excel template spreadsheets (.xlsx or .xls)
Raw sequencing data gzip-compressed FASTQ (.fastq.gz or .fq.gz)

All other files are generated internally by the platform and must not be submitted.

Metadata#

Metadata can be recorded in two ways:

  • Portal forms: registration of one record at a time (one patient, one sample or one experiment). Projects, Investigations, Studies and Assays are always registered through forms.
  • Template spreadsheets: registration of several patients, samples or experiments at once. Download the template from the registration page itself, fill it in and upload the file. Before saving, a preview shows the rows read and the errors found.

When using spreadsheets, use only the templates provided by ATPBR and keep their original structure (do not rename, remove or reorder columns). The filling rules for each field are the same as in the forms and are described in the Data dictionary.

Raw Sequencing Data#

Sequencing data must correspond to the primary files generated by the sequencing instruments.

Accepted modalities

In this first version, the following omics modalities are accepted:

  • Whole genome sequencing (WGS).
  • Whole exome sequencing (WES).
  • Gene panel sequencing.
  • RNA sequencing (RNA-seq).

Other modalities, such as DNA methylation, will be added in future versions.

FASTQ file requirements

  • Files compressed in gzip format (.fastq.gz or .fq.gz).
  • For paired-end reads, both files of each sample: read 1 and read 2.
  • Linked to an Assay: uploaded through the portal or located on the INCA Multi-user Bioinformatics Platform (see Data submission).
  • Names identical to those entered in the fastq_1 and fastq_2 fields of the Experiments.
  • File names free of personal data, such as the patient's name or medical record number.
  • Integrity verified by checksum.

Data Generated by ATPBR#

From the FASTQ files, the platform internally generates:

  • Alignment files (CRAM).
  • Somatic variants (VCF and MAF).
  • Gene expression matrices.
  • Copy number data.
  • Quality control metrics.

The pipelines that generate these files are described in the next chapter.
